Our Story

Petal & Wisp was born from a love of delicate botanicals and the quiet power of scent. Every fragrance is hand‑blended in small batches using ethically sourced oils.

We believe perfume should whisper, not shout — a gentle embrace that lingers. Our signature pink and brown palette reflects warmth, femininity, and earthy elegance.
